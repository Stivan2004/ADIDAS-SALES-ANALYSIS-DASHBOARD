# ADIDAS-SALES-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD
# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzes Adidas sales data to monitor total sales, profit, units sold, average selling price, and profit margin.

It provides insights into monthly sales trends, state-wise sales, region-wise performance, product-wise sales, and retailer-wise sales. Interactive filters allow users to explore sales data by month, sales channel, and year.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total sales and total profit.
* Track the total number of units sold.
* Monitor average price per unit and profit margin.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Compare sales performance across states and regions.
* Analyze product-wise sales performance.
* Evaluate retailer-wise sales contribution.
* Compare sales channels, including in-store, online, and outlet.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**1. Monthly Sales Trend**
Visualizes monthly sales performance and helps identify high-performing and low-performing months.

**2. State-Wise Sales Analysis**
Displays sales distribution across different states in the United States using a geographical map.

**3. Region-Wise Sales Analysis**
Shows the percentage contribution of different regions to total sales.

**4. Product-Wise Sales Analysis**
Compares sales across product categories, including men's and women's footwear and apparel.

**5. Retailer-Wise Sales Analysis**
Highlights the sales contribution of retailers such as Foot Locker, Sports Direct, Amazon, Kohl's, West Gear, and Walmart.

**6. Interactive Filters**
Allows users to filter data by month, sales channel, and year to explore sales performance.
